## Issue-filter methodology

Each included issue filter uses a controlled canonical key and neutral display label. It is selected only where **two evidence classes** are present: first, an official campaign platform page on a verified campaign domain; second, a defined neutral search-context query for Google News, Google Trends, and Autocomplete. No filter is represented as a claim about advertising, candidate preference, voter opinion, or issue salience. A missing reporting source is shown explicitly as an empty `reportingSources` array rather than reconstructed or inferred.

> **Portrait provenance rule.** Candidate image records contain source links only. A `verified_official_campaign` record requires the campaign page URL, direct image URL, and `sourceDomain` to exactly match the verified campaign domain. When that is not available, only a directly linked official `.gov` portrait may be recorded as `verified_official_government`; otherwise the record is `unavailable`. All retained image records require manual rights review and are marked `source-linked_not_independently_licensed`.

The test definitions use the Google Ads Transparency Center, Google News, Google Trends, and Google Autocomplete engines only. Google Ads Transparency queries are manifest inputs for observable delivery evidence. Google Trends values are meaningful only within an individual multi-query request; News and Autocomplete are noisy discovery/context surfaces and are not opinion measures. [3]
